### 
<h1 align="center"><u>Analysis of Retail Ad Campaign</u></h1>

A data-driven analysis to identify why ad spending wasn't translating into profit  and what to do about it.
 
---
 
## Problem
The business was spending heavily on ads but profits weren't matching up. Some campaigns caused massive losses with no early-detection process in place.
## Goals
To identify ad campaigns that is causing major financial losses. Test if high-loss campaigns still bring in strong revenue. Make data-driven recommendations for better ad budgeting.

## What I Did
 
- Performed **descriptive analysis** on impressions, clicks, CTR, conversions, cost, and revenue
- Ran **P&L statistical analysis** and checked for normal distribution and applied the Empirical Rule (68–95–99.7%)
- Used **Z-scores** to detect outlier campaigns (Z < −3) bleeding the most money
- Conducted a **hypothesis test** comparing revenue between high-loss vs. normal campaigns (p-value = 0.0165)
- Built visualizations for top 10 profitable vs. bottom 10 least profitable ad groups

  
  🔗 **[Check out full analysis on here](https://github.com/TUITOEKK/Retail-Ad-campaign-analysis/blob/main/Analysis.ipynb)**
 
---
 
## Recommendations
1.Pause or rework coupon-based campaigns, especially on mobile.  
2.Prioritize top performers like Black Friday/Cyber Monday and competitor-targeting campaigns.   
3.Implement monthly Z-score analysis to flag highloss campaigns early.   
4.Adopt performance-based budgeting allocate more to proven profitable ad groups.   
5.Introduce monthly A/B testing to continuously learn what works.
